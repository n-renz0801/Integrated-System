# Project MEASURE School Edition

**A desktop app that helps you fill out the DepEd Office Performance Commitment and Review Form (OPCRF) on your computer instead of on paper or in Excel.**

## What it does

- Has one tab for each of the four pages of the form (EOPCRF I to IV)
- Lets you type your names and details once; the other pages reuse them
- Does the computing for you and saves your work automatically
- Prints each page in the official layout, or saves it as a PDF using the **Save to PDF** button

## How to get it

1. Open the [Releases page](https://github.com/n-renz0801/Project-MEASURE-School/releases/latest) and download the latest `.exe` file.
2. Double-click it to start. Nothing else needs to be installed.

If Windows or your antivirus shows a warning the first time, that is normal for small programs. Choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**, or ask your IT person to allow it.
